Some groups might be composed of sub-groups such as dividing military or government information from civilian-accessible information and so therefore, those people and or devices with access to only part of the collected information could be absolutely understood to be never involved in analyzing or recording or storing any of the excluded or prohibited information, and additionally, because any number of topics or specific subjects might be prohibited for recording or observing or analyzing by any number of classes of people or devices, if they were not allowed to consider or record or obtain or analyze the information they could not be treated as if they had been capable of or involved in any analysis of it, and therefore would have to reasonably be excluded from any consideration which would require them to have had access to those capabilities or that information.
